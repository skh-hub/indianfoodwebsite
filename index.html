About Indian Food

Indian cuisine is one of the most diverse cuisines in the world. It is famous for its rich flavors, colorful dishes and variety of spices. Different regions of India have their own traditional foods, cooking styles and ingredients.

Popular Indian Dishes

🍛 Biryani

A flavorful rice dish prepared with spices, rice, vegetables or meat. Hyderabad is especially famous for its delicious biryani.

🥘 Butter Chicken

A popular North Indian dish made with chicken, tomato-based gravy, butter and aromatic spices.

🥞 Masala Dosa

A crispy South Indian dish made from fermented rice and lentil batter, usually served with potato filling.

🍲 Idli & Sambar

Soft steamed rice cakes served with spicy lentil-based sambar and coconut chutney.

Regional Indian Cuisine

North India

Famous for naan, butter chicken, paneer dishes, parathas and rich gravies.

South India

Known for dosa, idli, sambar, rasam and coconut-based dishes.

West India

Includes Gujarati, Maharashtrian and Goan cuisine, with dishes such as dhokla, pav bhaji and seafood.

East India

Famous for Bengali sweets, fish dishes, rice-based meals and traditional desserts.
